Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, all single-player — PowerPyx. Follow the stages in order; every trophy in a stage is listed under it.

Only 1 possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason's house in Chapter 1 (morning after Down to Earth). It is unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it; do it when it comes up and the risk is gone. Sources: PowerPyx roadmap, SixShore.

      Sources & method

      Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them, as tracked by Exophase and PSNProfiles (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden). Every guide is written in our own words for this guide and cites its sources beside it — a location or step with no source earns no credit, so none is given without one. Where the method was still unconfirmed at launch (18–19 Nov 2026), the guide says so instead of guessing. Launch-era facts come from PowerPyx's GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, cross-checked against GTABase, SixShore, WikiGTAVI and OutputLag.
